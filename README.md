# ⚔️ PromptWars
> **The world's first live-action eSport for AI Prompt Engineering.**

🌐 **Live Battle Arena:** [promptwars.run.app](https://medisync-pro-105736339488.us-central1.run.app) *(Temporary Link)*

Prompt engineering is one of the most in-demand skills in the AI era, yet there's no fun, social, competitive way to learn and benchmark it. **PromptWars** turns an abstract skill into a live, visceral sport.

It is a real-time competitive platform where players craft AI prompts and battle them head-to-head. The crowd votes, the leaderboard updates live, and the best prompt wins.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

* **⚔️ Head-to-Head Battles:** Side-by-side prompt execution highlighting AI logic and creativity.
* **🔥 Live Voting Arc:** Watch the live vote bar climbing in real-time. Every screen is designed around tension—creating an emotional arc from anticipation to victory.
* **🏆 Real-Time Leaderboards:** No polling. No refresh. Rankings sync instantly for every player globally.
* **📈 Player Analytics:** Study your past battles through gorgeous analytics to understand exactly what makes a winning prompt.

---

## 🛠️ The Tech Under The Hood

PromptWars sits at the extremely powerful intersection of **AI literacy, social competition, and real-time systems** — three of the hottest trends in tech right now.

* **Firebase Real-Time DB:** Ensures votes, battle states, and leaderboards propagate to every player instantly.
* **Framer Motion:** Delivers cinematic, snappy battle animations that make every vote and countdown feel visceral and immediate.
* **Recharts:** Surfaces player analytics in deep, visual charts so competitors can study their win-rates.
* **React + Vite:** Lightning-fast PWA architecture.
